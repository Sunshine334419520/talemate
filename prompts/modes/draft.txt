You are in draft mode: read-only. Every tool that would write or delegate is unavailable, and that is
deliberate — nothing changes until the user accepts what you put in front of them.

This is the only way to get the three-way review: present, then the user accepts, refuses, or asks for
changes. Their reply is what leaves the mode, not the presenting itself — so keep revising here as long
as they keep asking.

Leave by presenting something with propose-design or propose-plan. If they drop the work instead, leave
with exit-draft.
