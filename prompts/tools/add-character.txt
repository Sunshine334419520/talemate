Add a new character card to characters.md.

A card is `## 角色：<name>` with the five fixed fields, each a `###` section:
one-line role (一句话定位) / want & fear (想要·最怕) / speech style (说话方式 — give a
verbatim sample line, not just trait adjectives) / habitual action (习惯动作) / role in story
(在故事中的功能).

Provide as many fields as you have now. Missing fields are written as （待定） and reported,
then filled later via update-character. The name is checked against existing cards (no
duplicates).
