Present one chapter's plan for the user's approval, and end the turn. Nothing of the chapter is
written yet — this is the step before writing, and the turn ends here so the user can judge the plan.

Call it once the plan for ONE chapter is ready: yours, or the planner's when the material was not
already in hand. The plan is saved as the chapter's plan artifact, so the user's answer applies to
exactly the bytes they were shown; calling it again for the same chapter replaces that artifact.

Write a scene brief, not a step-by-step script. The prose writer needs room to invent blocking,
dialogue, timing and emphasis; the plan fixes what happens, not where the camera sits.

Write in Chinese, in exactly this shape:

## 依据
One line: which sequence function this chapter advances, and what changes (information, relationship,
resource, position, cognition).

## 前情
2–4 sentences. Only what the reader must know entering this scene: who these people are to each other
right now, what each of them wants, and what is already settled. Do not recap the whole book or the
world.

## 这场戏
One continuous scene: where and when, who is present, what each person wants, who blocks the
protagonist, and what is at stake. The protagonist must have a live person to act against, not only
an interior realization — even a quiet chapter needs someone in the room who wants something and
resists.

## 必须发生
3–6 bullets. Each is an event, decision or reversal with a person and an obstacle. Do not write camera
directions, word counts, “放大点”, or “第 1 步 / 第 2 步”.

## 不能提前兑现
What must stay unresolved, and which 禁揭 item this chapter must not touch.

## 章末问题
The question the chapter leaves open — an interrupted action, a discovery, a relationship shift, or a
new question. Do not force a cliffhanger.

## 产物（按序）
- design/characters/<名>.md for a new character, or design/wiki/<page>.md for a new world fact.
  These are listed first because they still land through propose-design, before the prose; never
  write prose around a missing prerequisite.
- The chapter file: chapters/chapter_ch<N>_v<M>.md.
- Only the state files this chapter actually moves (state/characters/<名>.md, state/foreshadowing.md,
  state/progress.md). state/progress.md is a window of the last 7 chapters, one section per chapter,
  two lines each.

No 题材, no separate 意图, no 价值变化 section, no step-by-step beats, no per-step word budgets, no
“放大点”, no execution red lines. Those were the old execution script; the brief replaces them.

The volume outline's 本卷在全局的位置, 本卷目标与阻力, 本卷的承诺 and 本卷的节奏与情绪 bound this
chapter; the sequence contract covering it says what this unit must make true. Read both before
writing the brief. If the material reveals a better path that changes the sequence's exit state or
promises, raise it in 前情 as a design question — the prose must not silently override the contract.

Plan text only: no notes to the user, no rationale, no questions — say those in your message before
this call.

After the user approves, carry the plan out yourself. If they ask for changes, revise and call this
again — a loop, not a one-shot.
