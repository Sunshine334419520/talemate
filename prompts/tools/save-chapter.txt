Save a finished prose chapter into chapters/. filename follows the naming rules:
chapter_ch<N>_v<M>.md. Use this for the actual prose you would rather persist to disk than
dump back to the writing partner.

Saving asks the user first, unless the edit permission says otherwise for this mode (see
docs/permissions.md).

This is for prose only. Beat plans are never saved anywhere: present them with propose-plan
and pass them straight to task(writer).
