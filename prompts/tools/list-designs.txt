List the project's design docs (design/) grouped by layer (core.md / wiki/ / characters/ /
outline/) with, under each, its section headings — so you know what material exists, how to
address sections, and which items are still placeholder （待定）. Call this before editing.

Character cards are one line each: `名字 · 身份（必有齐／待补：…；另有：…）`. 「待补」lists
required sections that are still empty; 「另有」lists the card's free-form sections beyond the
five required ones, so you can see what is on a card without reading it. Read the card itself
with read-design to get its content.
